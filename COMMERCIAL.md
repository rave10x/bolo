# Commercial license

Bolo is free for **personal, non-commercial use**. Commercial use needs a paid license.

## You need a commercial license if you

- use Bolo inside a company, startup, agency or any organisation that earns money,
- use it to train or coach employees, students or clients as part of a paid service (bootcamp, institute, tutoring, recruitment or placement business),
- charge people to use it, host it for others, or bundle it into a product or service,
- modify it or build something on top of it for any of the above.

## You do not need one if you

- are an individual learning English for yourself,
- use it for private study, hobby or personal projects,
- are a student or teacher using it without charging anyone and without any commercial purpose.

Terms are in [LICENSE](LICENSE) (PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0). If you are unsure, ask first.

## How to get a commercial license

Open an issue titled **"Commercial license"** at https://github.com/rave10x/bolo/issues/new?title=Commercial+license and say who you are, how you plan to use Bolo, and how many people will use it. You will get a price and a written license.

## Privacy

Bolo does not track its users and sends nothing anywhere, so commercial licensing relies on honesty. Companies that use Bolo commercially without a license are in breach of the license terms and the copyright holder may enforce them.
